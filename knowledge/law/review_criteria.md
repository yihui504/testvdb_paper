# Law Paper Review Criteria

## Evaluation Dimensions
### 1. Doctrinal Rigor (Weight: 40%): Correctly use legal provisions? Proper hierarchy of sources? Provisions cited with exact article numbers and text? Judicial interpretations correctly applied? Grey literature clearly distinguished? Scoring: 1-5
### 2. Analytical Depth (Weight: 25%): Multiple interpretive methods applied? Value tensions identified and analyzed? Doctrinal position justified with reasoning? Competing interpretations engaged? Arguments developed rather than asserted? Scoring: 1-5
### 3. Originality (Weight: 20%): Novel reading of existing provisions? Overlooked tension/gap identified? New analytical framework? Clearly distinguished from existing scholarship? Scoring: 1-5
### 4. Presentation (Weight: 15%): Footnotes only for citation sources? Academic register maintained? Title free of "keyword + colon" format? Quotation marks only for statute names and legal concepts? Logical structure? Scoring: 1-5

## Required Sections (Law Papers)
1. Introduction / 2. Institutional Background / 3. Normative Analysis / 4. Literature Review / 5. Comparative Analysis (optional) / 6. Conclusion and Recommendations

## Common Issues
**Major**: Arguments without statutory support, opinions in footnotes instead of body, misquoting statutory provisions, failing to address competing interpretations, treating scholarly opinion as equivalent to statutory authority
**Minor**: Quotation marks for emphasis, "keyword + colon" title, AI-pattern phrases, excessive grey literature, literature review that lists rather than analyzes

## Paper Types
### Doctrinal Analysis: Focus on interpretation of specific provisions, normative arguments. Must have: multi-method interpretation, value analysis, statutory citations.
### Comparative Legal Study: Focus on cross-jurisdictional analysis, transplant feasibility. Must have: fair presentation of foreign law, analysis of adaptability.
### Legislative Reform Proposal: Focus on identifying deficiencies, proposing amendments. Must have: current framework analysis, concrete amendment text, justification.
### Case Comment: Focus on analysis of a specific judicial decision. Must have: case facts, legal reasoning critique, broader implications.
