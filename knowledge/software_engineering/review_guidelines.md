# How to Write a Good SE Review

## Core Principles
1. **Take a stance**: If you have expertise, take a clear stance for or against. "Weak accept" and "weak reject" are for reviews outside your area.
2. **Clearly communicate reasons**: Make clear what arguments are important. Identify 2-3 key concerns if arguing against.
3. **Summarize key points**: Strengths/weaknesses fields should be short bullet lists. Write these at the end.
4. **Cover all review criteria**: Ensure all criteria are addressed.
5. **Try to be positive**: Give papers a fair chance. Distinguish fixable issues from fundamental flaws.
6. **Find the right length**: Negative reviews can be shorter. Positive reviews may have constructive suggestions. Borderline papers benefit from detailed feedback.
7. **Be deliberate about questions**: Only ask questions where answer might change judgment.
8. **Critique the paper, not the authors**: "The paper fails to explain" not "The authors fail to explain."

## Discussion Guidelines
A. Engage with substance / B. Argue your position / C. Be open to changing your mind / D. Don't delay
