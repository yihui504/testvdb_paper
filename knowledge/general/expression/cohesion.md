# Cohesion and Signalling

Cohesion is the glue that connects sentences and paragraphs. Without it, even correct sentences read as a disconnected list.

## Signalling Words by Function
### Addition: furthermore, moreover, in addition, additionally, also, likewise, similarly
### Contrast: however, nevertheless, nonetheless, in contrast, on the other hand, conversely, whereas, while, although, yet
### Cause / Effect: therefore, consequently, as a result, hence, thus, accordingly, for this reason
### Exemplification: for example, for instance, specifically, in particular, such as, notably
### Sequence: first, second, next, then, subsequently, finally, meanwhile (avoid "firstly/secondly/thirdly" in argumentative prose)
### Concession: although, even though, despite, notwithstanding, admittedly, granted
### Summary / Conclusion: in summary, to summarize, in conclusion, overall, taken together
### Reformulation: in other words, that is, namely, specifically, put differently

## Given-New Information Flow
Each sentence should start with **known** (given) information and end with **new** information.
- ✅ "The model was trained on ImageNet. **This dataset** contains 1.2M images." (given → new)
- ❌ "1.2M images are contained in **ImageNet, which** was used to train the model." (new → given)

### Patterns
- Topic chain: Same subject across sentences
- Derived topic: New sentence's given info derives from previous sentence's new info

## Lexical Cohesion
### Repeat key terms — don't use elegant variation
- ❌ "We trained a model. The neural architecture performed well. This AI system..."
- ✅ "We trained a model. The model performed well. The model achieves..."

## Paragraph-to-Paragraph Linking
### End-start bridge: Last sentence of paragraph N previews first sentence of N+1
### Transition sentence: Start new paragraph with explicit transition

## Common Pitfalls
- Transition overload: every sentence starts with "Moreover" / "Furthermore"
- False contrast: using "however" when there is no actual contrast
- Orphan paragraphs: connects to nothing before or after
- Elegant variation for key terms: cohesion killer in technical writing
