# Content Analysis Guide - Economics & Management

## Purpose
Extract and organize key information from research materials for empirical economics/management papers. Focus on hypotheses, data, methods, and theoretical grounding.

## What to Extract
### 1. Research Context: Theoretical Foundation (which theory: agency theory, resource-based view, institutional theory), Research Gap, Policy/Practical Relevance
### 2. Hypotheses: Variables (Independent, Dependent, Mediating, Moderating), Hypothesis Logic (theoretical reasoning connecting X to Y), Hypothesis Chain (logical consistency across H1, H2, H3)
### 3. Data and Methods
**Data**: Source (CGSS, CSMAR, Wind, CFPS, World Bank, Compustat, etc.), Sample period and size, Variable definitions and measurement, Data cleaning/filtering criteria
**Methods**: Baseline model (OLS, Logit, Probit, Fixed Effects), Endogeneity strategy (IV, DID, PSM, RDD, GMM, Heckman), Robustness checks, Mechanism tests (mediation)
### 4. Metadata: Target journal and tier, word/page limit, paper type, discipline

## Econ/Management Conventions
1. Variable consistency — Same variable name in text, tables, and equations
2. Economic significance — Report magnitude/meaning, not just p-values
3. Endogeneity awareness — Always anticipate reverse causality and omitted variable concerns
4. Data plausibility — Values must be logically consistent and realistic
5. Theory-driven — Every hypothesis must trace back to established theory
