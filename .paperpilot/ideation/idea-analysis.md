# TestVDB — Idea Analysis Report

> Generated by `paperpilot:ideation`, 2026-07-10. Evidence from in-session literature search (arXiv 2502.20812 / 2506.02617, VDBFuzz ICSE'26, MeTMaP FORGE'24, BUZZBEE USENIX'24, NoREC ICSE'20, DDLCheck VLDB'25) + recency re-check (2026-07).

## Problem–Solution Statement

**Problem.** VDBMS *incorrect-behavior* defects are the majority (43% of bugs, per the testing roadmap [arXiv:2502.20812]) yet lack practical test oracles. Crash oracles (VDBFuzz, ICSE'26) cover only 23%; no reference implementation exists for differential testing; and a naive LLM-as-oracle fails because the LLM both *generates* the contract and *judges* compliance — hallucinated constraints are self-confirmed (contract hallucination propagation; 25% of our submissions were judged by-design, i.e., the LLM-derived contract was stricter than maintainer intent).

**Approach.** TestVDB — (1) an LLM auto-derives a contract oracle from official API documentation; (2) **Contract-Truth Separation (CTS)** isolates the assertion layer (LLM contracts/judgments) from a truth layer (a dev-reviewer agent) that falsifies assertions via maintainer-authority evidence: clean reproduction, source-grounded verification, threat-model cross-check.

## SWOT

**Strengths**
- First LLM-driven realization for VDBMS API-compliance defects (niche verified open, 2026-07).
- Contract hallucination propagation = genuine finding, backed by 12 by-design cases (25%).
- Strong empirics: 111 issues, 36 acknowledged (28 fixed + 8 accepted), 5 VDBMS, precision 12.9%→69.2% (5.4×).
- Complementary to VDBFuzz by design (35/36 yield non-crash; near-zero overlap).

**Weaknesses**
- Method novelty incremental: doc-crawling + multi-agent debate are established techniques.
- dev-reviewer TP recall only 20–60% — a real capability gap.
- Crash excluded (complementary, not a full tester); soft result-correctness (ANN recall/ranking) unsolved.
- Cross-layer ablation is Milvus-only; Weaviate has 22 open undiagnosed.

**Opportunities**
- VDB-testing agenda just opened (roadmap 2025) — first-mover on the LLM realization.
- Contract-hallucination finding generalizes to other LLM-as-oracle domains (code review, compliance audit).
- 3-library overlap with VDBFuzz (milvus/weaviate/qdrant) gives a direct comparison basis.

**Threats**
- **Same research group owns the agenda** (Shenao Wang / Haoyu Wang line: roadmap + bug study + VDBFuzz) — scoop risk HIGHEST; they have motive, means, and data for the LLM step.
- Reviewer-pool overlap with that group at SE venues (ICSE/FSE).
- "Method not tech" framing lowers the ceiling at the most competitive SE venues.
- LLM variance: dev-reviewer unstable on the same candidate across runs (2/2 in pilot) — undermines reproducibility claims.

## Risk Assessment (Top 3)

1. **Scoop by the competitor group** (Shenao/Haoyu Wang line). They own roadmap + bug study + VDBFuzz and are the most natural next author of an LLM-driven VDB tester.
   *Mitigation*: submit early (VLDB rolling deadline / ISSTA, ahead of ICSE/FSE March cycle); monitor their outputs monthly until submission.
2. **TP-recall 20–60% undermines the "reliable oracle" claim.**
   *Mitigation*: frame as precision-focused (precision 5.4×, FP-removal 80.6%); run the recorded stronger-model re-triage to measure TP-retention vs FP-suppression; report recall honestly.
3. **Reviewer overlap at SE venues** (competitor group likely in PC).
   *Mitigation*: target a DB venue (VLDB) or testing-specific (ISSTA/ASE) to shift the review pool; sharpen differentiation vs VDBFuzz/roadmap in related work.
