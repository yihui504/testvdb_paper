Qdrant is a [vector database](https://qdrant.tech/documentation/overview/) and a [semantic search](https://qdrant.tech/documentation/overview/vector-search/) engine. You can use its REST API to develop a production-ready service to store, search, and manage vectors with an additional payload.

## How does Qdrant work?

  1. First, you should create a [collection](/v-1-12-x/api-reference/collections/create-collection) to store all your data.
  2. Then upsert data [points](/v-1-12-x/api-reference/points/upsert-points) and enrich them with a custom [payload](/v-1-12-x/api-reference/points/set-payload).
  3. With a full collection, run a [search](/v-1-12-x/api-reference/search/points) to find relevant results.
  4. Collections can be [snapshotted, downloaded and restored](/v-1-12-x/api-reference/snapshots/list-snapshots).
  5. When ready, setup a [distributed system](/v-1-12-x/api-reference/distributed/create-shard-key) for production.
